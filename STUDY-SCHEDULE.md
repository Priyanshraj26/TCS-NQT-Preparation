## Tips for Following This Schedule

1. **Don't skip days** — consistency is more important than intensity
2. **Track progress** — check off problems in Top 50 tracker
3. **Review mistakes** — maintain an error log
4. **Time your practice** — use a stopwatch for coding
5. **Sleep well** — 7-8 hours minimum, especially Week 4
6. **Stay hydrated** — keep water bottle at your study desk
7. **Adjust as needed** — if a topic is easy, move faster; if hard, spend more time
